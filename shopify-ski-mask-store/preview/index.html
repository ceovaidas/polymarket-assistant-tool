FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Does it fit under a helmet?

Yes. The crown is low-profile and sits comfortably under most ski and snowboard helmets.

Will it fit me?

One size fits most adults (head circumference 54–62 cm). The knit stretches and keeps its shape.

Is it warm enough for real skiing?

Yes. It's a full balaclava with a fleece-lined face panel — proper ski kit that happens to have a face on it.

Can I wear it with goggles?

Yes. The eye port is cut wide so goggles sit flush and your view stays clear.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship in 1–3 business days. Delivery usually takes 6–12 business days to the US, Canada and Europe, with tracking.

What if it's not for me?

Return it unworn within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Just email us with your order number.

How do I wash it?

Hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry. Don't tumble dry.
