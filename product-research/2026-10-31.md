### 1. Old-man-face balaclava
- Buyer: friend or partner, 18-35, buying a gag gift for the funny skier, snowboarder or "always cold"
  friend. Moment: they put it on and the whole group cries laughing; photos on the slope.
- Average TikTok Shop price is low (GMV / units = $12.16), so on Shopify sell the moment, not the mask:
  $19.99 single, 2-pack $29.99 ("99% off the second one" style decoy for "you + your clown").
- Hook: "DON'T let the clown of your ski trip see this 🤣" → GoPro POV on a chairlift, friend turns
  around wearing it. Also the 3-2-1 reveal and the store-laugh hook from `hooks.md`.
- Risks: copycats are already flooding in; season ends around February; check knit quality and the face
  print on a sample; never use a real person's face.

### 2. Tear-off microfiber cloth roll
- Buyer: home cleaners, "clean with me" crowd. Problem: paper towels streak and run out. Not a gift.
- Hook: "POV: you stopped buying paper towels" (tear, wipe a streaky mirror, done in 3 s).
- Risks: very new (one strong week), many sellers will copy; low price so margin comes from the 2-3 roll
  bundle. Recheck growth next week before ordering.

### 3. Sunset projection lamp (2nd gen)
- Buyer: teens / young adults redoing their room; gift for a sister or girlfriend.
- Hook: "My room at 9 pm vs my room with this 🌅" (before/after).
- Risks: second wave of a 2020 trend, electrical item (needs FCC/CE-marked supplier), more returns.

### 4. Pixel-art speaker
- Buyer: girlfriend or parent buying for a gamer. Hook: "Put your cat's face on a speaker 😭".
- Risks: battery shipping, app quality, margin thin if the cost is $20+.

### 5. Scalp massager brush
- Below the $15 floor alone, so only as a 2-pack or an add-on. Hook: "The 30 seconds my scalp waits for".
- Risk: no health claims (no "hair growth").

### 6. Mini label printer
- Supplier cost not checked yet; organise-with-me audience; app-dependent. Check before going further.

## Watched, not passed
- **Christmas LED light gift boxes, set of 3:** 5,367 units on Oct 17, then 32, then 1 a day; 217 LIVEs
  and 5 videos. A one-day LIVE spike, not a trend. Wait for a second strong week.
- **Generic squishy / fidget advent calendar** (NeeDoh's sold 6,313 since Oct 10): kids' toy, needs US
  toy safety certificates (CPSIA) and must ship before Dec 1. Skip.
- **Press-on nails** ($444,727 GMV): sizing returns and a crowded, brand-heavy niche. Skip.
- **Neck firming cream, Dyson fans, PS5 bundles:** brands, skincare claims or high ticket. Skip.
