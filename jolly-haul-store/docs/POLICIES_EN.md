## Refund policy

**30-day returns**
Not happy with your order? You can request a return within **30 days of delivery**.

- Items must be unused, unwashed and in their original packaging.
- Face masks and balaclavas are hygiene items: they can only be returned unworn, with any tags still attached.
- Sale items and gift cards are final sale unless they arrive faulty.

**How to start a return**
Email incvaidas@gmail.com with your order number and the item(s) you want to return. We'll reply within 2 business days with the return address and instructions. Please don't send items back before you hear from us.

**Return shipping**
Return shipping costs are paid by the customer, unless the item arrived damaged, faulty or not as ordered.

**Damaged, faulty or wrong items**
If something arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, email us within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or a full refund, including shipping, at no cost to you.

**Refunds**
Once we receive and check your return, we'll email you and refund the original payment method within 10 business days. Your bank may need a few extra days to show it.

**EU and UK customers**
You also have the statutory right to withdraw from your purchase within 14 days of delivery without giving a reason. This policy does not limit your rights under consumer law.

## Shipping policy

**Processing time**
Orders are processed within **1–3 business days**. You'll receive an email with a tracking link as soon as your order ships.

**Shipping rates**
- **Free standard shipping** on orders of $50 or more.
- Standard shipping on orders under $50: $4.99.

The exact shipping cost is always shown at checkout before you pay.

**Delivery times**
Most orders arrive within **7–15 business days** after shipping, depending on your location. Delivery estimates are not guaranteed and may be longer during the holiday season.

**Christmas delivery**
Order by **December 10** for the best chance of delivery before Christmas.

**Customs and duties**
Orders to some countries may be subject to import duties or taxes, which are the responsibility of the recipient.

**Lost or delayed parcels**
If your tracking hasn't updated for 10 days or your order hasn't arrived within 25 business days, email incvaidas@gmail.com and we'll help — including a reshipment or refund if the parcel is lost.
