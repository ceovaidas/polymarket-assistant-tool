What's inside

Everything between a viral clip and a posted video

SPLIT

Automatic scene detection

Finds every cut, pulls a clean start frame for each scene and keeps the original timing.

SWAP

Product swap that stays identical

Connect Shopify once. Your product is locked across all scenes, colours and angles.

CAST

Finds every person, you pick the new face

We detect each person in the video. Swap them for your own photos (with their permission) or a saved AI character you reuse across videos.

SET

New location per scene

Elevator, gym, ski lift, mall Santa. Or describe your own in one line.

HOOK

Hook library with live ranking

Text hooks sorted by this week's performance, burned in with TikTok-style captions.

A/B

Variants in one click

Same video, five different hooks. Post them all and keep the winner.

PARTS

Download by scene

Grab each scene or each start frame and finish it in CapCut if you prefer.

POST

Post straight to TikTok

Schedule to connected TikTok and Instagram accounts with the AI-content label set.
