# Instagram Reels: set-up, warm-up, posting, reading the numbers

Reply in Lithuanian. Source: the user's "Instagram Mastery" course notes (Notion, read 2026-10-31).
The view numbers and ratios below are that course's field experience, not official Instagram figures:
treat them as targets to test against our own results, and update this file with what our accounts show.

## 1. Set up the account (day 0)
- Fresh email: a Gmail at least 48 h old, or a new ProtonMail. One account per store / niche.
- No modded apps. Do not add a phone number or extra emails in the first days (fresh accounts that
  add everything at once get flagged).
- Switch to Professional: Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account → **Creator**, category
  **Video Creator** (or Public Figure). (A store can switch to Business later for the shop link and
  ads; start as Creator for reach.)
- Username, profile photo, bio and look must feel native to the niche at first glance
  (toetally.extra: yellow/black, funny, "for the clown of the friend group").
- Course mentions proxies/VPNs for US targeting; we do NOT rely on them (they cost account trust).
  Reach the US through sounds, hashtags, captions and location instead (section 5).

## 2. Warm up (days 1-3), no posting
Every day, about 1 hour of scrolling Reels inside the niche (funny gifts, pranks, gag gifts, clogs,
TikTok-made-me-buy-it):
- like about every 2nd reel, save about every 5th, follow about 5 niche accounts a day;
- the goal is a feed that is almost only the niche, so Instagram knows who the account is for.

## 3. Posting schedule
| Day | Reels a day |
|---|---|
| 1 (after warm-up) | 1 |
| 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 4 |
| from ~day 7 | up to 6 |
- About 2 hours between uploads.
- Use the SAME hashtag set every time (repeated niche association). Our current 5: see `hooks.md` /
  the toetally caption pack.
- "Lottery tickets": every strong reel is another chance to break out; 6 a day = 84 tries in 2 weeks.
  Volume only counts if each reel passes the hook checklist in
  `.claude/skills/video-prompts/templates/hooks.md`; reuse proven hooks with new situations.

## 4. What the algorithm looks at (and our targets)
- First push: roughly 130-170 viewers. At that stage **watch time** decides almost everything;
  engagement matters less than on TikTok.
- Targets per reel: **average watch time 9 s+ minimum, 12 s+ = strong** (with 11-15 s reels this
  means most people watch to the end or replay: hide the payoff until the last second, loop the end
  into the start).
- After the push: like:view about 1:12 or better, save:view about 1:70 or better.
- Healthy account: reels keep landing at 200-2,000 views. Repeated 0-100 views = problem (section 6).
- Ask the user for these numbers per reel (views, avg watch time, 3 s hold, likes, saves, shares,
  link clicks) and log what hook/format/model each reel used, so we learn what actually sells.

## 5. Reaching US viewers from Lithuania (honest ways)
- Sounds: only trending US sounds or sounds trending in the niche (Instagram groups reels by audio).
- Hashtags: niche tags + 2-3 US tags (e.g. #usagenz, #foryouusa, #americanmemes), same set each time.
- Location tag: a big US city (Los Angeles, New York, Miami) that fits the scene.
- Caption: explain the reel naturally in US slang and humour, with the buyer's own words.
- On-screen text and voices in US English; US settings in the AI shots (realism kit).

## 6. Shadowban / "0-view jail"
Signs: every reel under 100 views, several at 0, nothing in Explore/Reels, still so after 6-12 hours.
1. Stop posting completely for 3 days (keep scrolling and liking normally).
2. Check nothing broke the rules: reused watermarked videos, copyrighted music outside the app,
   trademarks (never "Crocs"), misleading claims.
3. Still dead after that: start a fresh account for the store, set up properly from section 1.
   Never use new accounts to get around an actual ban or removal for breaking the rules: fix the
   cause first, or the new account and the store's ad account go too.
What gets punished: spammy or aggressive posting, sudden jumps in activity, mass follow/unfollow,
bots, bought followers or engagement, suspicious log-ins.

## 7. Reply format when the user asks
Short day-by-day plan with their account name and niche filled in, the targets from section 4, and
what to send back (the numbers) so we can adjust.
