## Buyer facts found (use in copy, all true)
- The real pain: no water on lashes for 24-48 h after a lash lift or new extensions.
- Main objection to stick-on shields: they peel off if the skin is wet or oily, or when you tip your head
  back under the stream. Lashify's own instructions: apply to a dry, oil-free face.
- So our copy and videos must not show or promise more than the real product does. Before running ads, film
  a real test (dry face, press 10 s, full shower) and keep the claim to what that test shows.
