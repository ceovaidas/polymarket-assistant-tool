# Template: GoPro POV trunk reveal ("POV: the clown of your friend group restocked")

**Status:** ✅ proven, praised by the user (Oct 25 2026, toe charms for clogs "EXTRA TOES"). Worked first
try in two versions: an SUV liftgate in a suburban driveway, and a fishing pickup with a camper shell at a
lake boat ramp. Became the opener of two finished videos.
**Works for:** any boxed product, especially gag gifts and "restock" energy; the theme of the truck and
place sets the niche (fishing, hunting, camping, tailgate, Christmas).

## Why it works
- First-person hands = the viewer is the one opening it. A closed trunk opens a question at 0 s.
- The product appears only when the trunk opens (1.5-2.5 s), and there is a LOT of it: abundance plus
  "this is in stock" social proof. The hands lift one box to the lens = the product shot.

## Build (2 images + 1 Start/End clip, ~9 credits in the studio)
1. **Start image (no product reference attached):** chest-mounted GoPro POV, slight natural wide-angle,
   behind a closed {VEHICLE} in {PLACE}; both hands enter from the bottom edge ({SLEEVES}, a plain black
   wristband for continuity), one hand on the handle, the other on the edge, ready to open. No badges,
   no logos, no licence-plate text.
2. **End image = an EDIT of the start image:** "keep everything identical… the liftgate/shell window is
   now raised out of the top of the frame (tailgate folded down), the cargo area is packed with identical
   boxes from reference 2, fronts facing the camera, all the same size {+ THEME PROPS: rods, tackle box,
   cooler}; my two hands hold one box by its sides, lifting it toward the camera, window facing me".
   Attach: start image, box packshot, product photo.
3. **Video, Kling Start + End frame:** "One single continuous take, no cuts… 0-1.5 s opens; 1.5-2.5 s
   the cargo is revealed, the boxes were inside the whole time and never move by themselves; 2.5-5 s both
   hands grab one box and lift it toward the camera, ending exactly as the end image. Natural GoPro body
   movement only." Negative: "cut, boxes appearing from nowhere, boxes moving by themselves, different
   box design, extra hands, car logo, licence plate text".
4. Backup if Kling rushes: two clips (open → hands on the raised edge; then reach in and lift), cut on
   the reach.

## Edit notes from the finished videos
- Keep the trunk clip to about 4 s (open at 1 s, box at the lens by 3.5 s), then hard-cut to situations
  with the same character (fishing: steps into the boat → casts on the dock → holds the fish).
- The last shot carries the CTA, so it must run at least 2.5 s (a 0.9 s and a 1.5 s ending were too
  short to read "Link in bio").
