# Viral research log (toe charms / gag gifts), Oct 26 2026

Text notes only; no third-party videos are stored. Add a dated section after every research round.

## What the market already proves
- Crocs itself launched official toe Jibbitz (two packs of five, about $20, limited) and the press reaction
  was "vile, but I love it" (Highsnobiety, Trend Hunter). Disgust + delight is the emotion that spreads.
  => never use the Crocs name or logo; our angle is "toe charms for clogs", priced as a gag gift.
- TikTok search pages for "toes coming out of crocs" and "dedos para crocs" hold millions of posts in total:
  the topic has proven demand; the winners are reaction and "wearing it in public" clips, not product ads.

## What the hook data says (vendor studies, directional only)
- Show the result / reaction in the first 1.5-2 s; product showcase hooks lead in a 34k-clip sample (OpusClip).
- Early hold beats length: keep 80% of viewers to 3 s; minimal text, let the visual carry the joke
  (3,600-video study, thecontentlabs). Most viral clips burn in captions (OpusClip).
- Negative / contrarian framing often gets a higher hook rate ("Don't buy these…", "Worst gift I ever gave").

## Realism (AI clips must not read as AI)
- Image first, then animate (face holds far better than text-to-video).
- Phone-camera cues: handheld micro-shake, autofocus breathing, flat indoor light, "not commercial".
- Ordinary places and people, messy hair, home clothes; check hands and product shape per clip.
- Label realistic AI content and never present generated people as real customers in reviews.

## Our angle (different side of the same proven emotion)
1. Mission series: "Wearing toe clogs to places until someone notices — Day N" (story + recurring format).
2. Gift reaction: the unboxing face at 0.5 s, then the friend wearing them everywhere.
3. Contrarian hook: "Do NOT buy these if you have dignity 💀" → payoff wearing them at a serious place.
4. Comment bait that splits people: "Vile or genius? 👇" (the exact reaction the press had).
5. Real footage beats AI for this product once a sample arrives; AI for hooks and impossible places.
