# Idea library (what we learned from reference reels)

Text notes only. The reference videos themselves are third-party and are never committed.
Check this file before writing hooks, choosing a format or planning a batch of videos.

## Batch 1: 80 reels of one viral novelty brand (Oct 2026, user's Drive folder)

All 80 are ads for one product (a latex "middle finger" head mask) from one brand. The lesson is not the
product, it is **how they turned one shoot into ~90 posts**.

### The formula (every video, 6-21 s, median 14.5 s, 5-8 cuts)
| Beat | Time | What happens | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0-2 s | One of 4 openers (below) | Hook line, top third, 2-3 emojis 😂😂😂 |
| Build-up | 2-10 s | The product used in 3-5 everyday situations, 1.5-2.5 s each, real reactions | "Tag someone who needs THIS mask" |
| Payoff/CTA | last 2-3 s | Best reaction (laugh, double take) | "More info in bio" |

### Openers (first 1-2 s)
1. **POV hands unboxing** on a lap in a car or on a kitchen counter (product in a clear bag).
2. **"Fell off a truck"**: cardboard boxes scattered by a highway guardrail / a van dropping boxes, often
   an American flag in frame → hands open the branded box in the car. Pure curiosity, very American.
3. **Already wearing it** in a mirror / doorway (Halloween costume angle).
4. **Store shelf** full of the product, a customer picking one.

### Hook lines (swap the identity, keep the skeleton)
- "DON'T show this to [identity] 😂" (a road rage driver, an angry biker, someone who's over people,
  someone with NO costume, daily haters, impatient people, a Thanksgiving uncle)
- "DON'T let a [identity] see this 😂" (a Texas prankster, a Florida man, an American dad, a Midwest
  prankster, a PRO prankster, a prankster on the slopes)
- "This [product] feels illegal 😂" / "Wearing this feels illegal"
- "Everyone NEEDS this for [situation]" / "Your patience NEEDS this daily" / "This is SO necessary"
- "POV: You found your Halloween costume" / "POV: It's Monday and you need THIS" /
  "POV: It's FRIDAY at 4:59 PM" / "POV: I brought this to work"
- Comment-reply: a "Where can I get one?" comment sticker + "You guys keep asking where to get this" /
  "So many of you wanted this and now it's yours" (only with a real comment).

### Situations they rotate through (build-up clips)
Traffic and road rage, police stop (the cop laughs), drive-thru window, TSA / airport, plane aisle,
office meeting and Zoom call, ski lift and lodge, kitchen scare on a partner, Halloween party,
Thanksgiving family, car wash, gas station, mail carrier, leaf pile with kids.

### The real lesson: the variant machine
- They filmed ~25-30 clips once, then **recut them into ~90 posts**: new hook text + a different first clip
  + a different identity ("Florida man", "Texas prankster", "Midwest") + different music.
- Regional and identity targeting (US states, dads, bikers, office workers, skiers) makes each post feel
  personal → more tags and shares.
- Seasonal swaps on the same clips: Halloween → Thanksgiving → Monday/Friday office jokes.
- Music seen (Shazam on 10): *Can-Can (Offenbach)* ×4, *Free Bird* (cover) ×5, *Boombastic – Shaggy* ×1.
  Upbeat, instantly recognisable, comedic.

### How we apply it (cat bed, Eightlegs, Jolly Haul)
- **Generate a clip bank, not one video.** For a product make 8-12 AI clips (opener, 5-6 situations,
  best reaction close-up) once, then cut 10+ posts by swapping hook, first clip, identity and song.
  Far cheaper per post than generating every video from scratch.
- Cat bed: "DON'T show this to a Texas cat dad 😂", "DON'T let a Florida cat lady see this",
  "This cat bed feels illegal", "POV: It's Monday and your cat needs THIS", "Everyone's cat NEEDS this
  for road trips"; situations: truck cab, fishing boat, office desk, couch, Thanksgiving table,
  Christmas tree, camper van, vet waiting room.
- "Fell off a truck" opener works for any product: our plain shipping box on a highway shoulder → open
  it in the car → product. Never show another brand's logo or a real delivery company.
- Eightlegs: "DON'T let a biker see this jacket", "This jacket feels illegal", bike meet, gas station,
  night city, concert queue.
- Jolly Haul: "DON'T show this to a Thanksgiving uncle", "POV: you found the gift for the man who has
  everything".
- Honesty: comment-reply stickers only with real comments; reactions in AI clips must not be presented
  as real customers.
