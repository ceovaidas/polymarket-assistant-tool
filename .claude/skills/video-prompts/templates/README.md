# Viral template gallery

Proven formats from the user's projects. Before writing prompts from scratch, check this list. When the
user says a generated video worked, save it here as a template with slots (`{PRODUCT}`, `{PERSON}`, ...).

| Template | File | Status | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3-2-1 reveal → it goes everywhere | `countdown-reveal-everywhere.md` | ✅ proven (user-approved) | portable products, pet products |

Music identified in the reference reels (Shazam): 3-2-1 reveal → *Mambo No. 5 – Pérez Prado*;
fan-blade swap and "It's just a hoodie" → *Golden Brown – The Stranglers (slowed)*; cat-bed problem vs
solution → *Be My Baby – The Ronettes*. Organic posts: add the song from the in-app music library.
Paid ads: commercial-library tracks only.

Formats tried in conversation but not yet confirmed by a finished video (save them once one works):
- **Problem vs Solution:** night-vision problem shot, then a cozy product solution (cat bed, 74k-like reference).
- **POV single take:** a person puts the product on in one shot ("POV: you're a ___ lover").
- **Top-down fan-blade outfit swap:** the person lies on a rug, a ceiling-fan blade wipes and the outfit changes.
- **Truck restock chase:** the crew chases a moving truck, rips bags open, then a hero shot in another place.
- **Bed tease "Nope", then toss:** POV from the bed, the partner teases with the product and tosses it.
- **Couple shop drop:** the partner says "stop buying…", an item drops and smashes, the product is bought, then a hero shot.
