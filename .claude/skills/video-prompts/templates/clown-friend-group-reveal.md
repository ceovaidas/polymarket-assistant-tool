# Template: "DON'T let the clown of your friend group see this" (store laugh → reveal → situations)

**Status:** ✅ hook and situations liked by the user (Oct 25 2026, toe charms for clogs "EXTRA TOES").
Liked clips: the store hook (woman walks with a cart, stops, points, laughs, then the product), the
graduation stairs and the library POV feet on the table. Remixed from a 12 s laser-pointer reel
(warehouse laugh → graduation → night photoshoot → library, "Link to buy in bio").
**Works for:** wearable or visible gag products and novelty gifts (fake toes, masks, funny socks, hats,
costumes), anything that is funny the moment people see it in a serious place.

## Structure
| # | Time | Shot | Text (top third, same line until the CTA) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-3 s | Hook A, product hidden: {SHOPPER} pushes a cart down a warehouse-store aisle, stops, points out of frame, bursts out laughing. Static camera | "DON'T let the clown of your friend group see this 🤣😭" |
| 2 | 3-5.5 s | Hook B, reveal: the camera turns to a pallet stack of {PRODUCT} boxes (see "How to get the pan" below), she keeps pointing and laughing | same |
| 3-5 | 2.5-3 s each | Situations: someone wears/uses {PRODUCT} totally seriously in a serious place; people around notice and laugh. Static camera | same |
| last | 2.5-3 s | Payoff situation with the product biggest in frame (POV feet on the library table worked) | "Link in bio · mystery discount 🤣" |

Total 11-15 s. Hard cuts only. Make 3-4 videos from one clip bank: the same hook + 3 different situations each.

## Packaging (needed for the hook)
One packshot first, then attach it to every store shot: a bright lemon-yellow box with thick black
borders, doodles, a clear window showing the product, chunky lettering with the product name and a small
"fits your clogs"-type sticker. If the model garbles the lettering, re-roll or add the text in the edit.
Never print a third-party brand name (no "Crocs") on the box or in the copy.

## Shopper identity (paste identically)
American woman about 30, long straight platinum-blonde hair in a loose high ponytail, light tan skin,
natural makeup, small gold hoop earrings, light-wash denim jacket over a plain white t-shirt, black
leggings, white sneakers. (The first try with a muscular buzz-cut man also worked; the user preferred her.)

## How to get the store hook right (5 iterations it took)
1. v1: man laughing next to the boxes → the product was visible from frame 1, no curiosity.
2. v2: woman with the boxes behind her → still visible from frame 1, she stood still and pointed late.
3. v3: start frame with no boxes, motion "she points off-frame" → Kling slid yellow boxes into the frame
   and her hand was already on her mouth in the start frame, so the laugh had nowhere to build.
4. v4: start frame (walking, no boxes) + end frame (woman pointing at boxes, a different-looking image)
   → Kling inserted a hard cut at 1.5 s because the two frames looked like two places.
5. ✅ Fix: build the **end frame by editing the start frame** ("the same moment, camera turned about 60°
   to the right, the box stack 2 m away now fills 70% of the frame, the woman only partly visible at the
   left edge pointing"), then Kling Start + End frame with "one single continuous handheld take, no cuts,
   the friend swings the phone right following her arm and settles on the boxes". Backup: two clips cut
   on the same pointing action, or one wide 16:9 clip with the pan done as a moving 9:16 crop in the edit.

Start frame rules: she is mid-stride, neutral face, both hands on the cart, no product and no yellow
anywhere, the right edge is plain orange racking with brown boxes. Negative: "yellow boxes, product
appearing, objects sliding in, hand on mouth at the start, cut, jump cut, scene change".

## Image prompt skeleton (fill every slot; the product sentence is pasted identically every time)
```
REAL phone footage, NOT CGI, shot from knee height about 2.5 metres away, natural 1x phone lens, no
wide-angle distortion, in {PLACE}. Full-body shot: {WEARER, age, hair, clothes, action, serious face}.
On his/her feet a pair of fully closed plain black rubber clogs from the product reference at a normal
realistic adult size, exactly proportional to the body (classic perforated clog shape, rounded closed
toe cap, round holes on top, black heel strap, no logo badge anywhere), with five small realistic
skin-tone silicone fake toes sticking out of the front holes on top of each clog, normal human toe size,
natural creases and pale pink nails, ten fake toes in total, the wearer's real feet hidden inside, NOT
sandals, clearly visible and sharp. {WHO ELSE, where}. Setting: {DETAILS}. Composition: portrait 9:16,
{WEARER} filling 80-85% of the frame height, the clogs in the lower fifth, deep focus, slight handheld
tilt. Lighting: {LIGHT}. Colour: {PALETTE} with skin-tone toes, natural phone colour science, high
micro-contrast, subtle noise. Photorealistic faces and fabric, small real-life imperfections, no phone
visible anywhere, no text, no logos, no watermark.
```
Negative: `oversized shoes, giant clogs, clown shoes, huge feet, fisheye, sandals, bare feet, real toes, logo badge, pink`

## Motion skeleton
```
Continue exactly from the start image, same full-colour footage, same lighting and white balance,
single continuous take, no cuts, no close-up insert. Locked-off static camera: no zoom, no push-in, no
orbit, no pan. {WEARER} does {ONE serious action} with a completely straight face, feet stay still.
{WITNESS} glances down at the shoes, double-takes and laughs / tries not to laugh. The clogs stay
exactly as in the start image: black closed clogs with skin-tone toes sticking out on top; the toes are
rigid and never wiggle, nothing changes shape or colour, no logo appears. Sound: {AMBIENCE}, laughter.
```
Negative: `toes wiggling, sandals, bare feet, different shoes, logo badge, different colour, camera movement, cuts, morphing, extra legs, extra feet, new person appearing`
Use 0.3-2.8 s of each 5 s clip.
