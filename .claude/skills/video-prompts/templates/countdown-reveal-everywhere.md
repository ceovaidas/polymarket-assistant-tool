# Template: "3-2-1 reveal → it goes everywhere"

**Status:** proven. User-approved generation, Oct 2026 (cat bed at a baseball stadium, 13.5 s, every
shot kept the same man and cat).
**Source format:** Peek Pod reel "POV: you're a cat parent with adult money now 😭" (2k likes);
our remix replaced the cage with a cozy product and the stair-rolling with safe arm-carrying.
**Works for:** any product that can be revealed under a cloth and shown "coming along" to places:
pet beds, plush, bags, blankets, jackets, gadgets.

## Structure (hook → build-up → payoff)

| # | Time | Shot | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–5 s | Person in a public seat holds a towel over the seat next to them, counts 3-2-1 on fingers, pulls the towel: product revealed already in use | Hook + reveal |
| 2 | 5–7.5 s | Same person at a pre-event spot (parking lot / tailgate) with the product on the chair next to them | "it comes everywhere" |
| 3 | 7.5–10.5 s | Walking through a crowd, carrying the product securely in both arms | Portability |
| 4 | 10.5–13.5 s | At the event, product on lap / own seat, reacting to the action; closer shot to end | Payoff, product as hero |

Music (original reel): **Mambo No. 5 – Pérez Prado** (1949 instrumental, its "one, two, three... uh!"
count matches the finger countdown). Add it via the Instagram/TikTok music library for organic posts;
for paid ads use a similar track from the Meta Sound Collection / TikTok Commercial Music Library.

On-screen text (whole video): `POV: you're a [pet/product] parent with adult money now 😭`
End CTA: `Comment "[WORD]" ...` (only with a real DM automation; otherwise "Link in bio").

## Slots to fill every time

- `{PRODUCT}`: exact description from the product photo: colours, print, materials, opening and lining.
  Use the **same sentence in every shot**.
- `{PERSON}`: age, hair, beard, plain clothes, **no logos**.
- `{PET_OR_USER}`: e.g. "a fluffy silver tabby cat with green eyes" (if the product is for a pet).
- `{VENUE}`: generic stadium / arena / festival / café, **no team or brand logos**.

## Shot 1 – start image
```
REAL iPhone 17 Pro Max photo, NOT CGI, vertical 9:16, {VENUE} in late-afternoon light, eye-level from the
next seat, slightly handheld. Deep focus, everything sharp, natural full colour, subtle real-life
imperfections.
{PERSON} sits in a stadium seat grinning at the camera, holding the corner of a plain white bath towel
draped over something on the seat next to him. Rows of fans in plain caps around, upper deck and bright
sky behind. Warm low sun, natural shadows, slightly blown-out sky. Photorealistic faces, fabric and
crowd, no phone visible near the camera, no team logos, no text, no watermark.
```

## Shot 1 – motion (3-2-1 reveal)
```
Continue exactly from the start image: same man, same seat, same towel, same stadium and crowd, same
full-colour light, same camera angle, slightly handheld, single continuous take, NO cuts, real-time speed.
0–2.5 s: he grins into the lens, raises his free hand and counts down with his fingers — three, two,
one — clearly mouthing "three… two… one…", the other hand holding the towel corner.
2.5–3.5 s: on "one" he pulls the towel off in one quick smooth move and lifts it out of frame.
3.5–5 s: revealed on the seat: {PRODUCT} with {PET_OR_USER} already inside/using it, calm, head
peeking out, blinking at the camera. He points at it proudly and laughs; fans behind lean in and smile.
It is already in place when revealed — it does not jump, run or climb. One pet only. The product keeps
exactly the colour, print and shape of the product reference. No colour or lighting change, no phone,
no logos.
Sound: crowd murmur, his voice counting "three, two, one", towel whoosh, an "aww", beat drop on reveal.
```

## Shots 2–4 – key wording that worked
- Parking lot: "sits in a folding camping chair... on a second camping chair {PRODUCT} with
  {PET_OR_USER} peeking out... portable grill, tailgating groups, golden hour".
- Carry: "holding {PRODUCT} securely in both arms against his chest, one forearm supporting it from
  underneath like carrying a baby in a blanket... He never holds it by the loop or with one hand."
- Event: "{PRODUCT} resting on his lap... the pet follows the action with its eyes... crack of a bat,
  crowd cheer".
- Every motion prompt starts with the lock line: "Continue exactly from the start image, same camera
  and colour, no cuts" and ends with "One pet, it stays in/on the product".

## Rules learned for this template
- **Colour drift:** the bed shifted between teal and blue across shots. Paste the identical
  `{PRODUCT}` sentence into every image prompt, attach the same product photo to every shot, and add
  "exact colour #XXXXXX" (sample the hex from the product photo).
- Never show the product used in a way it isn't designed for (a bed is not a carrier, so no
  one-hand or loop carrying, and no closing it with a pet inside).
- Never animals rolling or falling; never real team, venue or brand logos.
- Generate shot 1 first, then attach that person and pet as references to all other shots.

## Product-swap examples
- **Eightlegs jacket:** reveal the jacket folded on the seat (front up), then he wears it at the tailgate,
  walking the concourse, cheering in the stands. Text: "POV: you finally bought the jacket 😭🕷️".
- **Any plush or blanket:** reveal a kid or dog wrapped in it on the seat.
